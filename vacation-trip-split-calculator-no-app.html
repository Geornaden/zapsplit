Frequently Asked Questions

How does debt minimization work?

Instead of everyone reimbursing each individual expense (which creates dozens of back-and-forth payments), ZapSplit nets everyone's total spend against their share. Those who paid more than average receive money directly from those who paid less, reducing 15 transactions down to 2 or 3.

Can my friends edit the trip expenses on their phones?

Yes! By using the live ZapSplit edge app, anyone with the group link can view balances, add items, or tap to settle up instantly.
