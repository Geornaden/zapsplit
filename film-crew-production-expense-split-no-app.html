Frequently Asked Questions

How do indie filmmakers and commercial crews split shoot budgets without payroll software?
ZapSplit lets producers, directors, and department heads enter the total client shoot fee, deduct shared production expenses (like location permits, catering, and mileage), reimburse crew kit fees, and calculate net day rate payouts for every crew member instantly with zero subscription fees.
How do kit fee reimbursements work when crew members bring their own equipment?
Each crew member providing gear (camera package, sound mixer kit, lighting & grip, or drone) enters their kit rental fee. ZapSplit reimburses this amount directly to that crew member on top of their labor day rate, ensuring equipment wear and tear is covered.
Can we split travel per diems, mileage, and location rental costs?
Yes. Add any out-of-pocket production expense, record which crew member covered it upfront, and ZapSplit will balance the books so every dollar spent on gas, food, or studio rental is reimbursed before calculating net profits.
Do crew members need to create an account or download an app?
No. ZapSplit runs 100% inside any mobile or desktop web browser. You can generate a clean shoot payout wrap sheet and share it instantly via WhatsApp, Slack, iMessage, or email.
